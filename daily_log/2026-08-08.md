## Decisions and direction

Today's commit records several concrete design decisions:

- **Multidimensional arrays are now scheduled, not aspirational.** They have a version (0.5) and a design gate (0.4). The commit explicitly states this is a "language-level goal" independent of any particular application's needs.

- **The `:` collision is identified as the first-order blocker.** In Cx arrays, `:` already carries two meanings — type (`[3: t8]`) and index (`a:[2]`). Any multidimensional syntax has to live with both. Nothing downstream can be locked until this is settled. This is the clearest statement yet of why multidimensional syntax is genuinely undecided.

- **Row-major, last-index-varying-fastest** is the recorded layout decision, with the explicit framing that a future change would need to be a "deliberate reversal with a stated reason, not a drift."

- **2D and 3D are first-class**, not a stepping stone. This is recorded as a design commitment.

- **The existing array spec documents (`cx_arrays.md`, `cx_4d_arrays.md`) are design intent only**, and their syntax must not be retrofitted. Neither is in this repository.
