## Landed today

**fix(parser): don't promote a trailing builtin call to ret_expr** (`b6310fe`, 15:20 EDT) — CONFIRMED. Closes known-issues #2. A bare `print(x)` as the last statement of a function (no trailing semicolon) was being promoted to `ret_expr` by the parser's `func_body` combinator, routing it through `lower_expr`'s generic Call path which has no builtin dispatch — producing `unresolved semantic artifact reached lowering: function 'print'`. The fix adds `is_statement_level_builtin_call()` to `src/frontend/parser.rs` to recognize `print`/`println`/`printn`/`assert`/`assert_eq` and exclude them from implicit-return promotion. Left as a normal body statement, the existing `lower_stmt` builtin dispatch handles them correctly. No changes to the interpreter or IR lowering were needed.

Actual fixture conversion count was 5 (not the originally estimated 8): `t29_forward_decl`, `t31_strref_forward_combined`, `t67_macro_outer_test`, `t68_macro_outer_deprecated`, and `t_array_elem_arg_in_range`. Two originally predicted fixtures were never SKIP (they reject at macro processing), and `t50_nested_func_no_leak` remains SKIP for a separate nested-function limitation. One new fixture `t_print_trailing_position.cx` added. The fix was architecturally notable: a JIT lowering failure solved entirely in the parser, without touching the lowering layer.

**docs: known_issues.md #2 fixed** (`c05d154`, 15:21 EDT) — CONFIRMED. Updated `docs/known_issues.md` with the complete fix narrative, exact before/after fixture counts verified by diff, and the `t50` isolation finding.

**fix(semantic): scope if/while/loop/while-in bodies** (`603aa61`, 17:22 EDT) — CONFIRMED. Closes audit finding 3.1. Added `push_scope()`/`pop_scope()` calls around bodies of `if`/`else`/`else-if`, `while`, `loop`, and `while-in` in `src/frontend/semantic.rs`. Variables declared inside these control-flow bodies were previously visible after the block ended — a scope-leak bug. Four new test fixtures: `t179_if_scope`, `t180_while_scope`, `t181_loop_scope`, `t182_whilein_scope`.

**fix(semantic): enforce width-range check at method-call args, reassignment, array-index assignment** (`2d9a70b`, 18:04 EDT) — CONFIRMED. Closes audit finding 3.2. The existing `check_semantic_num_fits()` was only enforced at initial variable declarations and standalone call arguments. Now also enforced at three additional sites in `src/frontend/semantic.rs`: variable reassignment (~line 434), array-index assignment (~line 514), and method-call arguments (~line 1750). Six new test fixtures: in-range/out-of-range pairs for reassignment (`t_reassign_*`), index-assignment (`t_index_assign_*`), and method-call args (`t_method_arg_*`).
