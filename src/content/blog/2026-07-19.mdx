## What is sitting on submain

**Parser and semantic fixes** from the audit findings (3.1 through 3.3 and known issue #2): scoping corrections for if/while/loop/while-in bodies, width-range enforcement at method-call arguments and reassignment, ordering comparison rejection on Bool and Enum types, and the trailing builtin call promotion fix. These are correctness patches that tighten the language semantics in places the audit identified as loose.
