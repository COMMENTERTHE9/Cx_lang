The case for merging is strong (12 commits, including audit closures, interpreter fixes, and a design spec), but timing is the developer's call.

The other predicted items from yesterday also didn't land: cross-module coherence resolution, known issue #3 (explicit return plus trailing expression), and 0.3.3 design verification. None of these are blocked. They're waiting on the developer's attention to return to active development.
