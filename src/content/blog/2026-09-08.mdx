## The blocker: `copy_into` and container lowering

The reason the `.copy` work stopped at Slice 2 is `copy_into`. The `copy_into` parameter kind needs `SemanticType::Container` to map to an `IrType` before it can lower. That mapping does not exist yet. This is blocker #3 in the 0.4 roadmap and the single clearest resumption point when development picks back up.

The `t54_post_split_verify` fixture still SKIPs because of this. The other blocked fixtures (`t10` through `t13`, `t50_nested_func_no_leak`) are blocked on nested function definitions, a separate issue that causes JIT exit 127.

The `.copy` ABI and container lowering remain the clear entry point. When development resumes, that is almost certainly where it picks up.
